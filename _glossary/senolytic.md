title: "Senolytic"

A senolytic is an agent that selectively kills senescent cells, rather than merely suppressing their secretory output. Senescent cells survive partly by leaning on anti-apoptotic proteins, so inhibitors of BCL-2 and BCL-xL such as ABT-263 (navitoclax) and ABT-737 act as senolytics, as does the combination of dasatinib and quercetin.

Other approaches target proteins displayed on the surface of senescent cells, including CAR-T cells directed against such markers. ABT-263 has been evaluated clinically in advanced solid tumors, including in combination with gemcitabine, where thrombocytopenia is its characteristic dose-limiting toxicity.
